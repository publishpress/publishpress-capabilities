# How to Use Redirects

[PublishPress Capabilities](https://publishpress.com/links/capabilities) has a “Redirects” screen that allows you to control where users are sent before and after logging in to your site. There are four options available:

- **Login Redirect**: Where users are sent when they log in.
- **Logout Redirect**: Where users are sent when they log out.
- **Registration Redirect**: Where users are sent when they register on your site.
- **First Login Redirect**: Where users are sent when they log in to your site for the first time.

![PublishPress Capabilities Redirects Screen](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-capabilities/redirects-subscriber-3203a4588f.webp)

The “Login Redirect” tab allows you to choose from two options:

- Redirect users to the URL they were viewing before login.
- Redirect users to a specified URL.
