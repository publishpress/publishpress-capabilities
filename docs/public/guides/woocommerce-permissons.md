# Control Permissions in WooCommerce

[The PublishPress Capabilities plugin](https://publishpress.com/capabilities/) enables you to control permissions for the WooCommerce plugin.

- Go to the “Capabilities” link in your WordPress admin menu.
- In the main area of your screen will see options for WooCommerce Products, Orders and Coupons. These permissions cover editing, deleting and reading.

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-capabilities/woocommerce-capabilities-aa8e4fe2ea.webp)

In the right sidebar, you can also enable permissions for WooCommerce taxonomies.

- Check these boxes for Product categories, Product tags, and Product shipping classes:

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-capabilities/woocommerce-taxonomies-capabilities-0624c7509a.webp)

After you check those boxes and refresh your screen, you will see Manager, Edit, Assign and Delete options for Product categories, Product tags, and Product shipping classes:

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-capabilities/term-taxonomies-detailed-4f04c0ae90.webp)
